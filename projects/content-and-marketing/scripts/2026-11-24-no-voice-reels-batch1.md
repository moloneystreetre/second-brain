## 1. Price vs market cap (Freshman)
**IG/FB caption:** Share A costs ₦500. Share B costs ₦5. Which company is bigger? 🤔

Most people pick A, but the share price alone can't tell you. Market cap (price × number of shares) shows what the whole company is worth on the market. In our example, B is 10 times bigger.

Price is the cost of one slice. Market cap is the size of the whole cake. 🍰

## 2. Is a ₦2 stock cheap? (Sophomore)
**IG/FB caption:** "It's only ₦2, it must be cheap!" Not so fast. 🛑

Cheap means a low price compared with what the company earns, not a low number. In our example, a ₦2 share earning ₦0.05 costs 40 times its earnings (a P/E of 40). A ₦200 share earning ₦25 costs just 8 times (a P/E of 8).

But a low P/E can also be a warning sign, so always check why. 🔍

## 3. Dividend dates explained (Freshman)
**IG/FB caption:** Bought the share but missed the dividend? 😩 It all comes down to 3 dates:

1️⃣ Qualification date: be on the register by then (buy early enough for your trade to settle)
2️⃣ Closure date: the register closes
3️⃣ Payment date: the money lands, straight in your bank if you have an e-dividend mandate

And there's no free money: share prices often drop by about the dividend once the share goes ex-dividend.
