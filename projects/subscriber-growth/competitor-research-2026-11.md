# Competitor research and growth plan (24 Nov 2026)

Sources: vidIQ channel search, outlier search (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok), and the public pricing pages of Stears, NGN Market and Nairametrics. Figures are as of 24 Nov 2026.

## The short version

The NGX creator space is busy but shallow. Most of the big performers sell "X Nigerian stocks to buy before they explode". MoloneyStreetRe can't do that (education only) and shouldn't want to. That leaves the clear, data-led, compliant lane largely empty, especially for the diaspora.

Our YouTube channel already has 1,590 subscribers, 192 videos and 86k views, so if watch hours reach 4,000 we can apply for the YouTube Partner Programme. That's worth checking in YouTube Studio this week.

## Who's out there

**Research platforms.** Stears charges ₦4,000 a month, ₦10,000 a quarter or ₦35,000 a year in Nigeria, and $10, $27 or $99 abroad. Its data reports sell for ₦250,000 a year. NGN Market is freemium: free prices and alerts, then $9.99, $19.99 or $29.99 a month for stock picks, insider-dealing data and AI questions. Nairametrics gives the news away and earns from its paid stock research (Follow The Money), the Nairalytics data platform, sponsored posts and a Wealth Academy. Proshare also has paid plans, but its pricing page couldn't be read.

## What performs

1. **Breaking news, explained within 24 hours.** The Dangote Refinery IPO in September produced dozens of 500k to 2.9M-view reels, many 50 to 800 times the creators' usual views.
2. **Money you may already be owed.** Unclaimed dividends reels got 726k (84x) and 715k (18x) views.
3. **"What really happened" history with a twist.** "₦135k in Dangote Cement in 2010, but did devaluation eat it?" got 862k views (562x). "This man bought Dangote shares in 2007" got 1.5M views (865x).
4. **Step-by-step how-tos.** These included the FGN Savings Bond (478k, 189x) and how to invest in NGX stocks from anywhere on your phone (14x on YouTube).
5. **Short beginner series.** Cowrywise's 2-3 minute "Stocks made simple" episode 1 has 404k views (651x).
6. **Short weekly news briefs.** QuidMetrics' 2-minute "Nigeria Brief" got 17x.
7. **"X stocks to buy" listicles.** They are everywhere and get 2-6x, but they are buy calls, so we don't make them.

## How they make money

The money comes from cheap courses and masterclasses on Selar (dozens of "Nigerian stock investing" courses and replays), paid 2-day workshops, books, mentorship groups, and broker sponsorships or affiliate deals (Bamboo, Cowrywise, Meritrade). Platforms earn from subscriptions and data, with sponsored content on top.

## Gaps MoloneyStreetRe can own

1. **The trustworthy one.** We can be data-led, sourced and compliant, with no hype and no tips, in a feed full of "stocks that will explode".
2. **The diaspora.** Yinka is in London. "How to invest in NGX from the UK" (accounts, FX, getting money back, tax basics) is barely covered, and the one video near it got 14x.
3. **Naira-adjusted returns.** Hardly anyone shows real returns after inflation and devaluation, and it goes viral when they do.
4. **A daily data rhythm.** Nobody owns a 60-second "today on the NGX" show. We already pull the data every night.
5. **A levelled path.** Our Freshman-to-Senior levels can become a structured beginner series, like Cowrywise's, but NGX-deep.
6. **Practical money-recovery guides.** These cover unclaimed dividends, CSCS accounts, e-dividend mandates and bond sign-ups.

**2. A steady weekly rhythm.** This uses what we already have:
- **Mon-Fri:** the nightly 60s data reel (already running).

- **Within 24 hours of big NGX news:** a "Breaking, explained" short, with a ready-made template so it's fast.

**3. Ride the calendar.** Full-year results and dividend season (February to April 2027) lands right on the deadline. Plan a "Dividend Season" series from late January.

**4. Borrow audiences.** Pitch collabs or guest spots with mid-size NGX creators (Nigerian Investor and Femi Akinwale size). Post in diaspora groups and on LinkedIn, where UK-based Nigerians are.
