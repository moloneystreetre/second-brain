# Yinka's script writing style

Learned from Yinka's own edits to the four long-video scripts on 27 Nov 2026 (drafts vs Yinka's versions in `OneDrive\PA MoloneyStreetRe\scripts\long-videos\`). Use this for every script from now on. Where it differs from the generic brand voice, this wins.

## Openers
- Don't use "Sit down small, let's talk money." Yinka rewrote it both times. Use Yinka's own:
  - "Come sit down a minute for a quick money talk."
  - "Eh, have a seat and let's have our money talk, quickly."
  - "Moloney Street, good morning." (kept as it is)

## Sentences
- Fuller, explained sentences, not clipped fragments. "First, growth." became "First, is growth. You put ₦1 million in, you added nothing..."
- Talk to the viewer directly with "you": "You put in ₦1 million", "Are you chasing high yields?"
- Use linking words to carry the listener: "However,", "So,", "Now to...", "on the other hand,", "Now look closely."
- Hedge figures the way people speak: "about", "around", "in the region of", "thereabout".
- Turn a list heading into a question where it fits: "One. Are you chasing high yields?"

## Pidgin and local flavour
- A light touch of pidgin in the hook or at a turn: "Patience now! We go get there.", "By the end of this video, you go understand."
- Personify the villain: "Mr. Inflation".
- Don't lean on one food or scene in the hook: "No big grammar. Just simplicity." (not "Just suya").

## Names in examples
- Use everyday, neutral names that travel across Nigeria and the diaspora: Mama Joy, Uncle Peter, Judith, Bayo. Avoid stacking names from one ethnic group.

## Jargon
- Spell out an abbreviation the first time it's spoken: "Price to Earnings (P/E)", "Central Securities Clearing System (CSCS)".

## Money in the spoken script
- In spoken lines write amounts as words the voice will say: "2 million Naira", "20,000 Naira", "50 Naira". (On-screen cards can still use ₦.)

## Practical detail
- Add the real-world step people need: e.g. dividends are no longer paid by cheque, so complete the e-dividend mandate with the registrar through your broker.

## Unchanged (Yinka kept these)
- The five-beat / chapter structure, the leaking bucket, "Small small, the bird builds its nest", the sign-off "Know the stock before you buy the story. See you on Moloney Street.", all compliance lines.

## Watch-outs when reviewing Yinka's text
- Fix only typos and facts; never smooth out the voice. Tell Yinka what was changed.
