# Approvals (buttons, not typing)

Whenever Yinka needs to approve something (posts, reels, scripts, comment replies, anything going public), make it a one-tap decision:

1. Post the drafts or files in one message, with attachments or links.
2. Straight after, post a decision card (`request_decision`) with these options:
   - **Approve**: "Schedule/post exactly as shown."
   - **Change something**: "I'll ask what to change, update the drafts and send them back for approval."
   - Optional third option when it fits, e.g. **Approve all but #N** or **Skip tonight**.
   Recommend Approve only when the drafts passed the compliance check.
3. A tap on Approve is the go-ahead. A typed "approve"/"yes" counts too. Never post without one of them.
4. On "Change something", ask in one line what to change, redo the drafts, and send a fresh card.
5. If cards aren't available in the session, ask for a one-word reply ("approve" or what to change).
